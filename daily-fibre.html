How to take it

Too much fibre too fast can cause gas and bloating, so the first week is gentle.

  1. Start with half

    Days 1 to 7: stir half a sachet into a full glass of water (about 240 ml) and drink it straight away.

  2. Then one a day

    From day 8: one sachet a day, mixed the same way.

  3. Keep water close

    Drink plenty of water through the day. Take it at least 2 hours apart from any medicines.

Before you start

Always take it with a full glass of water

Taken dry or with too little water, psyllium can cause choking or a blockage.

Who shouldn't take it

Don't use it if you have difficulty swallowing, or a narrowed or blocked food pipe or bowel. Psyllium can cause allergic reactions in sensitive people.

Check with your doctor first if…

You are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes, or take regular medicines. Fibre can slow how medicines are absorbed, so keep at least 2 hours between them.

What you might notice at first

Some gas or bloating in the first days is common when you add fibre. Follow the gentle-start week and drink plenty of water.
