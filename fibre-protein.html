Fibre Protein

Protein and fibre in one scoop, for high-protein diets.

Gym-goers fill their plates with protein and often leave less room for whole grains, fruit and vegetables. Fibre Protein blends whey or plant protein with a fibre blend, so your daily shake covers both.

Key ingredients

  • Whey or plant protein
  • Fibre blend
  • Natural flavour

Who it's for: Gym-goers, athletes and anyone on a high-protein diet.

Formula, flavours and price will be confirmed before launch, so details may change.

Why you'll like it

  • Two in one shakeYour protein and part of your daily fibre in the same scoop.
  • Choose whey or plantWhey for most people, or plant protein if you avoid dairy.
  • Built for training daysFits after a workout or with breakfast, with no extra steps.
  • Mixes in a shakerBlends into water or milk without clumping.

How to use it

Final directions will be printed on the pack.

  1. Scoop

    Add one scoop to water or milk in a shaker.

  2. Shake

    Shake for 15 to 20 seconds until smooth.

  3. Drink

    Have it after training or with breakfast. Keep drinking water through the day.

Good to know

Check with your doctor first if…

You are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes, take regular medicines, or have trouble swallowing. Fibre can slow how medicines are absorbed, so keep at least 2 hours between them.

Start slowly

Adding a lot of fibre quickly can cause gas and bloating. Start with a smaller amount and drink plenty of water.
