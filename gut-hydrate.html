Gut Hydrate

Fibre and electrolytes in one clear drink, for long desk days.

Long hours at a desk, quick meals and too little water make it easy to miss your fibre. Gut Hydrate is a stick-pack drink mix of resistant dextrin, a fibre that dissolves clear, with electrolytes. Tear, pour into your bottle and sip through the workday.

Key ingredients

  • Resistant dextrin (soluble fibre)
  • Electrolytes
  • Light natural flavour

Who it's for: Young professionals and anyone with long desk days.

Formula, flavours and price will be confirmed before launch, so details may change.

Why you'll like it

  • Dissolves clearResistant dextrin mixes into water without grit or thickness, so it tastes like a light drink.
  • Fibre plus electrolytesTwo daily habits in one glass: your fibre and your hydration.
  • Made for the office bagSlim stick packs that fit a laptop sleeve or a pocket.
  • Easy to sipA light flavour you can drink slowly through the morning.

How to use it

Final directions will be printed on the pack.

  1. Pour

    Empty one stick into a glass or bottle of water.

  2. Shake

    Stir or shake until it turns clear.

  3. Sip

    Drink it through the morning, and keep drinking water through the day.

Good to know

Check with your doctor first if…

You are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes, take regular medicines, or have trouble swallowing. Fibre can slow how medicines are absorbed, so keep at least 2 hours between them.

Start slowly

Adding a lot of fibre quickly can cause gas and bloating. Start with a smaller amount and drink plenty of water.
