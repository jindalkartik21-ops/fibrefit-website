Using Daily Fibre

Start with half a sachet a day for the first week, then one a day, always in a full glass of water. Keep it 2 hours apart from medicines.

How to take it

Is it right for me?

If you're pregnant, nursing, have diabetes, take regular medicines, or have trouble swallowing, check with your doctor before you start.

Read the science

Something doesn't feel right

Some gas or bloating in the first days is common. If you have chest pain, trouble swallowing or breathing, or stomach pain after taking it, stop and see a doctor straight away.

Can I take it with my medicines?

Fibre can slow how medicines are absorbed. Take Daily Fibre at least 2 hours apart from medicines, and ask your doctor or pharmacist what's right for you.

Is it a laxative?

No. Daily Fibre is a fibre supplement meant to sit alongside a fibre-rich diet. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
